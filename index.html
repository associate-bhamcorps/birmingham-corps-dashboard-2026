📋 Pre-Program Survey — Questions 1–8

Charts and response data will appear here once participants submit the pre-program survey. The following questions are tracked:

Q1 — Household Barriers

Multi-select: unemployment, housing instability, food insecurity, transportation, caregiving, legal matters, health challenges, and more.

Q2 — Annual Household Income

Income bracket at time of enrollment — No income through $50K+, including prefer not to say.

Q3 — Reason for Joining

Multi-select: employment help, skills/training, mentorship, crisis support, referral, tried elsewhere, no other option.

Q4 — Prior Help-Seeking

Had they tried to access help before? Helped somewhat / Not right fit / Didn't know resources / Faced barriers / First time.

Q5 — Age Range

Self-reported bracket: Under 18, 18–24, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54, 55–64, 65 or older.

Q6 — Gender

Self-reported: Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say.

Q7 — What Does Success Look Like?

Open text — participant goals in their own words. Displayed as individual response cards.

Q8 — Biggest Challenge Right Now

Open text — what brought the participant to Birmingham Corps. Displayed as individual response cards.

📊 Post-Program Survey — Questions 1–4

Charts and outcome data will appear here once participants submit the post-program survey. The following questions are tracked:

Q1 — Program Effectiveness Rating

1–5 star rating: To what extent did this program address the challenge that originally brought you here?

Q2 — Outcomes Achieved

Multi-select: employment, housing stability, credentials earned, financial improvement, skills gained, community resources, mental health, network built, self-advocacy, family situation.

Q3 — Life Area Ratings (1–5)

Eight domains rated: Employment Status, Financial Stability, Housing Stability, Mental Health & Well-Being, Family Stability, Access to Resources, Social Support, Confidence & Self-Advocacy.

Q4 — Net Promoter Score (0–10)

How likely are you to recommend Birmingham Corps to someone facing a similar situation? (0 = Would not recommend, 10 = Would absolutely recommend)
